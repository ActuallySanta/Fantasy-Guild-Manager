## Headline

- **Deaths are down from 19-25 per run to 0-9** for every persona except the reckless gambler (31). Those deaths now come from failed quests and from heroes sent out already hurt, as designed. Players don't know the rule, though, and several called these deaths "unexplained".
- **Round 1 found the standing gates (10/40/100/200) blocking average players.**
  - Failures and denials now cost standing, so the typical, reckless and cautious players stayed at standing 11-133.
  - Their buildings stuck at level 2-3, and the Barracks capped the roster at 6.
  - With only 6 heroes, the 8-hero Demon King couldn't be fully staffed: one run lost at 47% and another won at 42%.
- **Tuned for round 2:** standing gates to **5/15/30/50**, and difficulty drift to **0.015 a day**. The typical player then reached level 5 buildings and won the 8-hero final at 79%. Mid-game odds sat at 50-100%.
- **Bug fixed:** the final could be denied, after which the run rolled on into day 51 with no ending. `HallRules.can_deny` now blocks that everywhere.
- **Bosses on days 10-40 can still be denied for about −2 standing.** That makes a boss cheaper to skip than to lose. This is a design gap, to be decided separately.

## New-player and UX confusions (rules mode)

- **No unassign** (severity 3). A hero in a party can't be removed and a party can't be cleared, so the only fix for a wrong party is to deny the quest.
- **Hidden role locks** (severity 2-3). Neither the shop nor the inventory shows which role can use an item. Every run wasted gold on gear nobody could wear.
- **Denying silently costs standing.** In one run standing fell from 45 to 7 on rest days.
- **Vague rejections:**
  - "gold or max level" was shown when the real block was the standing gate.
  - "role, slots or dead".
  - "gold or roster full".
- **Heroes in training or the Hospital are "busy"** with no reason shown in state.
- **Sent hurt, then died on a win:** a hero sent out Hurt can die on a won quest. The "At risk" tag shows this on Assign Party, but the bridge state has no matching field.
