## Headline

- **Every full run won with no real danger.** All four rules runs won both the Bone Drake (97-119% chance, a party of three) and the day-50 final. None of them came close to Game Over.
- **The economy runs away.** All four buildings were at L5 by days 25-27, and gold then climbed to 103k-128k with nothing to buy. Gold also piled up unspent on days 5-9, because upgrades stay locked until day 10.
- **Hero death is the only real pressure, and the game never shows it.** Runs lost 8-25 heroes, mostly on quests that succeeded at 90-150% displayed chance. Solo heroes and injured heroes died most.
